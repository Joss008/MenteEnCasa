# Critique: Mente en Casa Landing Page

## Design Specificity Verdict

**HIGHLY SPECIFIC — grounded in this product.**

The design is inseparable from "Mente en Casa" psychology home-visit service:
- Custom SVG logo with brain-tree-heart motif — unrepurposable
- Arch-shaped photo frames evoke therapeutic warmth
- 3-step orientation wizard purpose-built for psychology triage
- Every WhatsApp pre-filled message mentions "Mente en Casa" + "Trujillo"
- Coverage grid hard-coded to Trujillo districts
- Color palette (sage green + cream) semantically tied to "calma" and "bienestar"

**Unrelated product using it unchanged?** No. Every section, component, and copy block is product-locked.

## Emotional Journey

**Peak moments:**
1. **OrientationTool** — transforms passive browsing into active participation. The wizard's 3-question flow converts uncertainty into direction. This is the emotional peak.
2. **ProcessSection step 2** — "Te enviaremos el perfil del psicólogo colegiado asignado" addresses the #1 anxiety at exactly the right moment.
3. **CoverageTrujillo** — finding your district = emotional confirmation.

**Emotional valleys:**
1. **ServicesSection density** — 7 cards × ~300px = ~2100px of scroll before OrientationTool. Users who came uncertain must process too much before the tool designed for them.
2. **OrientationTool Q1 paradox** — the tool positioned as a simplifier starts with 6 simultaneous choices.
3. **WhatsAppModal friction** — district, time, and notes before sending at highest-intent moment.

## Strengths

1. **OrientationTool is the hero component.** Transforms passive browsing into active participation, reduces "I don't know what I need" anxiety unique to psychology services, generates personalized WhatsApp message. Genuinely differentiated UX pattern.

2. **Trust architecture is excellent.** CPsP credentials, promise to send psychologist profiles before visit, "Línea 113" emergency disclaimer — every trust objection preempted. ProcessSection step 2 is a conversion-destroying objection handled in one sentence.

3. **Emotional intelligence in copy.** "El bienestar emocional comienza en el espacio donde te sientes más seguro" doesn't sell — it validates. The entire copy layer respects vulnerability without being patronizing.

## Priority Issues

### P0 — Dual green palette creates brand confusion
- **What:** #3B5242 primary accent vs #496251 in WhatsAppModal/FloatingWhatsApp. Logo uses yet another pair (#3D614A, #23526E).
- **Why:** For psychology service where visual consistency = trust, 3 different greens undermines "profesionalismo"
- **Fix:** Standardize to #3B5242 as single sage green across all components
- **Command:** `rg -n "#496251|#3D614A|#23526E" src/` then replace

### P1 — OrientationTool Q1 has 6 options
- **What:** Tool positioned as simplifier but first question presents 6 simultaneous choices
- **Why:** Cognitive load degrades beyond 4 options. Q2/Q3 have 4 each — only Q1 exceeds threshold
- **Fix:** Collapse Q1 into 3 options: "Niño/Adolescente" | "Adulto" | "Familia o Evaluación"
- **Command:** Edit `content.ts` ORIENTATION_QUESTIONS[0].options

### P2 — Section ordering buries highest-converting component
- **What:** OrientationTool is section 5 of 8. Users who arrive uncertain must scroll past 11 cards before reaching the tool designed for them.
- **Fix:** Move OrientationTool directly after Gallery, before Services
- **Command:** In App.tsx, swap `<OrientationTool>` above `<ServicesSection>`

### P2 — WhatsAppModal friction at conversion moment
- **What:** Modal asks for district, time, and notes before sending. 3 decision points at highest-intent moment.
- **Fix:** Add "Enviar directo" link that bypasses the form. Keep modal as optional enhancement.
- **Command:** Add `<button onClick={handleDirectSend}>Enviar sin configurar</button>` in WhatsAppModal

### P3 — 20 lines dead CSS + font-weight transition no-op
- **What:** `.wizard-step-*` classes unused. `.nav-link` transitions font-weight which is not animatable.
- **Fix:** Delete dead CSS. Remove font-weight from transition property.
- **Command:** Edit index.css

## Persona Red Flags

### Jordan (Parent seeking child therapy — urgent, anxious)
- Must scroll through Gallery + 7 service cards before reaching OrientationTool
- Hero CTA is generic ("Deseo consultar disponibilidad") — doesn't mention children
- **Suggestion:** Add secondary hero CTA: "¿Necesitas ayuda para tu hijo?" with child-specific message

### Riley (Adult with anxiety — vulnerable, low energy)
- Page is ~8 sections (~12,000px+). Riley may not have energy to scroll to OrientationTool
- **Suggestion:** Add "skip to orientation" link in hero or floating "¿Necesitas orientación?" badge after gallery

### Casey (Arranging care for elderly parent — skeptical, verification-driven)
- Credentials (AboutPsychologists) is section 4, Coverage is section 7 — separated by 3 sections
- **Suggestion:** Add "Ver cobertura y credenciales" micro-CTA in hero

## Questions to Consider

1. If the OrientationTool converts 30% of visitors, why is it section 5 of 8? Is the page designed for the business or the user?
2. Does the modal's message preview build trust or create anxiety at the moment of conversion?
3. The emergency line (Línea 113) is buried in the footer. Should crisis resources be visible in the hero?
4. Seven services is a lot for a single-location practice. Can you cut 2-3 and increase conversion on remaining?
5. No testimonials or team photos — deliberate ethical choice (privacy) or missed trust-building opportunity?
